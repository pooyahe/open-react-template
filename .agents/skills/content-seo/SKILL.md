1. Treat `docs/CONTENT.md` as the approved copy baseline.
2. Use formal German `Sie` language and short concrete sentences.
3. Keep the page understandable without technical background.
4. Preserve one clear H1 and logical heading order.
5. Use keywords naturally; never stuff them.
6. Generate metadata from typed verified configuration.
7. Omit unknown values from structured data instead of emitting placeholders.
8. Reject fabricated metrics, testimonials, clients, certifications, partnerships, and guarantees.
9. Keep legal pages clearly marked for review.
10. Ask `ux-content` for a read-only review when a substantial content change is proposed.
