# SEO and Compliance Guidance

## Search intent

Use natural German language around:

- Digitalisierung für kleine Unternehmen
- Dokumentenmanagement
- Dokumente digitalisieren
- KI-Beratung für kleine Unternehmen
- Geschäftsprozesse automatisieren
- Datenanalyse und Dashboards

Do not repeat keywords unnaturally.

## Metadata placeholders

- Site title: `[COMPANY_NAME] | Digitalisierung für kleine Unternehmen`
- Description: `Dokumentenmanagement, KI-Automatisierung sowie Datenanalyse und Dashboards für kleine Unternehmen.`
- Canonical base URL: `[SITE_URL]`

## Structured data

Only include verified values for:

- Organization name
- URL
- address
- phone
- email
- geographic service area
- social profiles

Omit unknown values rather than publishing placeholders in JSON-LD.

## Legal boundaries

- Impressum content must be supplied and legally reviewed for the actual entity.
- Datenschutz content must match the actual hosting, forms, analytics, fonts, media, and third-party providers.
- Do not state “DSGVO-konform” as an absolute guarantee.
- Do not load analytics or external embeds before the required consent.
- Prefer self-hosted assets where practical.

This document is implementation guidance, not legal advice.
